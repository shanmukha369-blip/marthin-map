    SCIENTIFIC EXPLANATIONS FOR ASTRONAUT CREW

    [THIN ATMOSPHERE] Mars atmospheric pressure (0.61 kPa) is 160 times thinner than Earth (101.3 kPa). Lungs cannot absorb oxygen without mechanical pressurization.
    [EBULLISM DANGER] Body temperature (37°C) exceeds the boiling point of unpressurized water on Mars. Unprotected crew face instant bodily fluid boiling.
    [RADIATION EXPOSURE] Mars lacks a protective global dipole magnetosphere. Galactic cosmic rays (GCR) deliver ~0.68 mSv/day, requiring shielded habitat shelters.
    [DUST STORMS] Electrostatic dust storms can expand globally within days, attenuating sunlight by 99% and eroding suit optical seals.
